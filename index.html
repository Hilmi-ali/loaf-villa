A stay, proofed like dough.

We started with a simple rule: a good room, like a good loaf, needs time, few ingredients and a steady hand. Our linens are washed in small batches, our walls are limewashed, and breakfast is baked on site before you wake.

No lobby music. No lanyards. Just a key, a quiet bed and someone who remembers how you take your coffee.

    Good to know

    How do I reserve?+

    Add a room and any extras to your reservation, then send the request. We confirm by email within a day. Payment is made on arrival.

    Can I cancel or change dates?+

    Yes. Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Date changes are free, subject to availability.

    Are pets and children welcome?+

    Children of all ages are welcome, and Villa Oven suits families best. Small pets are allowed in the villas only.

    Is there a quiet time?+

    Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 07:00. The courtyard pool closes at 21:00.

    Can you cater to dietary needs?+

    Vegetarian and vegan options are available. Tell us about allergies when you reserve and the kitchen will plan around them.
